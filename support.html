About the app

Permit Notebook is a free Android study app for the Florida learner permit knowledge test. It has practice questions, mock exams, a review list, a driving log and a test-day checklist. It is written to be read by teens and parents.

Permit Notebook is an unofficial study aid. It is not from or affiliated with the Florida Department of Highway Safety and Motor Vehicles (FLHSMV), the State of Florida or any government agency. Questions are written in our own words from the Florida Driver License Handbook (revision 08/2023).

Common questions

How do I report a wrong question?

After you check your answer in a practice session, tap Report this question under the answer. It opens your email app with a message already started. The message holds the question ID, the app version and the handbook edition. Write what is wrong at the bottom and send it. If your phone has no email app, the app shows the question ID so you can copy it and email it to us later.

During a mock exam this button is not shown, so it cannot take you out of the exam. After the exam, open the missed-question review: it has the button.

Where is my progress kept? How do I delete it?

Your progress, driving log and settings stay on your phone. There is no account. We do not have a copy, so we cannot delete it for you.

To erase it: open Settings, then Your data, then Delete my progress. The next screen lists what will be erased, and you confirm there. Your settings stay. You can also clear the app's storage in Android's app settings, or uninstall the app. A copy may remain in your phone's Google backup until its next backup. The Privacy Policy explains this in section 7.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

Open Settings, then Privacy and ads, then Ad privacy choices. In places where Google does not need to ask, that row is turned off and says so. The Privacy and ads group also has a Privacy policy row.

How do reminders work?

The study reminder is off until you turn it on. In Settings, turn on Study reminder and pick a time. Once a day, your phone shows a notification with no sound. To stop it, turn Study reminder off in Settings. If you said no to notifications on your phone, Settings has a button that opens your phone's settings so you can change that.

What is the home-screen widget?

The widget shows the question of the day. Tap it to answer the question in the app. It is added from your phone's widget list, and the steps differ by phone. It has no answers and no ads.

How do I make the text larger or reduce motion?

The app uses your phone's text size. In Settings, under Display, the Text size row opens your phone's settings. Also under Display, Reduce motion turns off screen slides and fades. Its choices are Same as my phone, On and Off.

How do I share my driving log?

Open Stats, then Driving log, then Share log. You see the exact text first. You can switch on a spreadsheet file (CSV) if you want one; it is off each time. Then tap Share and pick an app from your phone's share sheet. The log is your own record, not an official FLHSMV form. The app holds no name, so none is in the text.

What do I bring to the real test?

Open Study, then Test-day checklist. It lists what to do and bring, with handbook page numbers, and you can tick items off. It covers your first-driver course, a parent or guardian if you are under 18, original identification, proof of your social security number, two documents that show your Florida address, glasses or contact lenses if you need them, and the fee. Rules and fees can change, so check with FLHSMV before you go. FLHSMV's list of what to bring is at flhsmv.gov/WhatToBring. The official handbook is here: Florida Driver License Handbook (PDF).
